# Entry 1: Deciding on my Topic (Nail Designing)
##### 10/24/26

## Why I choose this topic?
I chose nail designing as my topic because I love doing my nails. But I believe that technology can be incorporated into designing nails and it could make nail designing much easier. So, I looked into some articles which are [Futuristic Nail Art: How AI is Revolutionizing the World of Manicures](https://www.beautylaunchpad.com/nails/art/trends/article/22917820/futuristic-nail-art-how-ai-is-revolutionizing-the-world-of-manicures), [How Technology is Transforming Nail Art: Trends, Tools & The Future of Digital Beauty](https://techhubdigital.com/technology-transforming-nail-art-trends-tools-future-digital-beauty/), and [The Future of Nail Technology: Innovations Shaping the Industry](https://dollanailspro.com/blogs/news/the-future-of-nail-technology-innovations-that-are-shaping-the-industry). These websites showed many ways that technology can help nail designing like using AI to check what patterns look nice or making machines that make the nail designing process faster. This makes it easier for people like me and others that like nail designing.
